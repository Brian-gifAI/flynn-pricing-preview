How Flynn runs your GST and BAS, end to end

Flynn runs automatically on the schedule your firm sets for each client. It works through five review steps in order and only stops when something needs a person, then emails your team the exact issue.

1

Bank reconciliation and cash coding

Flynn finds unreconciled transactions and compares the statement balance with Xero. Through Aero Copilot in Xero, it suggests bank matches and cash codes uncoded transactions using each client's history. It also analyses the client's bank statement history, spots repeating payees and transactions, and suggests bank rules for your team to set up.

2

Code check

Flynn reviews every line's account and GST code against the client's coding history, flags mismatches, and writes the fixes you accept back to Xero.

3

Manual journal

Flynn finds over-claimed GST on entertainment, motor vehicles and home office, calculates the private-use adjustment, and prepares the journal for Xero.

4

Fixed asset document review

Flynn checks that high-value asset purchases have their supporting invoices attached.

5

GST variance check

Flynn reconciles the return against the balance sheet and aged receivables and payables, then explains any variance by breaking it into its parts (unpaid GST, late claims, timing differences and payments), each backed by the transactions behind it.

✓

Then, the workpaper.

Once every step is clean, Flynn prepares a branded workpaper pack in Excel and PDF, with the reconciliations, reports, review notes and a full audit trail. That's the outcome you pay for.
